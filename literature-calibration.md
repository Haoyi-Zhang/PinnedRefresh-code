# Full-text literature calibration

This matrix records the 12 same-venue, 5 influential, and 5 adjacent-paper reading slots used to calibrate the manuscript. APSS is intentionally counted in both the influential and adjacent sets, so the 22 slots contain 21 unique full texts. Chorus is maintained separately as an official metadata/abstract plus public-artifact record; its complete paper text was not available here, so it is not counted as a full-text calibration item. The matrix supports interface separation and writing calibration; it does not establish a global priority claim.
