# Reference audit

The manuscript contains **77 unique BibTeX entries**, and all 77 are cited in substantive prose or a comparison table. The final audit found no duplicate key, normalized title, or DOI; no missing cited key; no uncited bibliography entry; and no BibTeX compilation warning. Citation occurrence counts and the verification basis for every entry are recorded in `reference-audit.csv`.

This inventory distinguishes bibliographic integrity from source-reading depth. A `fresh-metadata-cross-check` records a current repair-round check of formal or primary metadata. `structured-source-calibration` and `recorded-source-depth` reuse the row-specific access record in the literature/resource ledgers. `structural-canonical` means that the entry passed syntax, uniqueness, citation, and compilation checks but was not individually re-queried online in this repair; it must not be represented as a fresh independent metadata verification.

The final repair corrected four material metadata defects rather than preserving a compilable but inaccurate list: the complete five-author byline and pages/DOI for Herzberg et al. (1997); the pages/DOI for Frankel et al. (1997); the pages/DOI for Wong, Wang, and Wing (2002); and the final Journal of Cryptology record and correct Boyle--Segev--Wichs byline for *Fully Leakage-Resilient Signatures*.
